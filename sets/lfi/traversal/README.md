# LFI traversal steps

These files contain one parent-directory step per line. Depth is generated by the bounded `repeat`
transform; pre-expanded values such as `../../../../` do not belong in the source sets.

`steps_all_linux.txt` and `steps_all_windows.txt` are broad aggregates for mass fuzzing. Linux uses
slash-based variants; Windows includes both slash and backslash variants because either may be
accepted or normalized by the target stack. They also decompose separator-only ideas from SecLists
`LFI-Jhaddix.txt`; targets, terminators, pre-expanded depths, and malformed source lines are not
copied. “All” means all reviewed built-in and Jhaddix-derived variants, not every possible traversal
representation.

Targets, suffixes, wrappers, and complete LFI payloads remain separate. SecLists LFI collections
are review or direct-use wordlists rather than atomic inputs.
